A growing collection of reasons — historical, philosophical,
scientific — behind the Christian faith. Written in the first
person, out of love, for anyone curious enough to look.

If you have a specific question, [honest questions](/questions/) is
the entry point. If you came in cold, the recommended path is
[gospel](/gospel/) → [history](/history/) → [origins](/origins/) →
[mind & meaning](/mind/).

The sections, in the order they build:

- **[The Gospel](/gospel/)** — the message at the center
- **[Origins](/origins/)** — universe, earth, life
- **[History](/history/)** — Jesus and the resurrection as a
  historical case
- **[Mind & Meaning](/mind/)** — consciousness, morality, suffering,
  meaning
- **[Honest Questions](/questions/)** — entry by the question you
  brought

The site is in active construction. I am writing it slowly, one piece
at a time, and the pieces here now are first drafts that I expect to
revise. Pieces are dated only by their git history; if you want to
follow the work, the repo is linked at the top of every page.

One last note: this site is *not* an attempt to win an argument. It
is an attempt to lay out, carefully, the reasons I find Christianity
to be true — and to do that in a way that respects you enough to give
you the actual evidence, not the slogan version. I would rather you
walk away unconvinced after seeing the real case than convinced by a
weak one. The real case is, in my reading, much stronger than the
slogans. But you can decide that.
