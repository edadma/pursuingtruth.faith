Most people do not arrive at a site like this asking *please give me
your systematic case for theism*. They arrive with a question. A real
one. Something they have been carrying for a while, or that came up
recently, or that they have just never gotten a satisfying answer to.

This section is organized around the questions I have heard most
often, in roughly the order people ask them. Each piece is a short
entry-point that points to the longer treatment in another section
of the site.

If your question isn't here, or if the one that's closest isn't quite
the one you meant — that's fair, and I would like to know about it.
The site is in active construction, and this list is meant to grow.

The questions, in no particular order:

- [Is there any real evidence for the resurrection?](/questions/evidence-for-the-resurrection/)
- [Doesn't science contradict the Bible?](/questions/science-and-the-bible/)
- [Why would a good God allow suffering?](/questions/suffering/)
- [What about other religions?](/questions/other-religions/)
- [Haven't I heard this all before?](/questions/havent-i-heard-this/)
